Chief Mobiliser — Busoga Region

Eng. Kizza Isma Kinyiri leads BRPV as Chief Mobiliser for the Busoga Region, providing strategic direction for regional mobilisation, volunteer coordination and community engagement across all districts of Busoga.

Leadership Responsibilities

  • Providing strategic direction for BRPV across the Busoga region
  • Coordinating regional mobilisation activities and volunteer engagement
  • Representing BRPV within the wider Patriotic League of Uganda structure
  • Overseeing district-level leadership and coordinators
  • Engaging with communities and stakeholders across Busoga
