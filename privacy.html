Privacy Policy

How Busoga Region Patriotic Volunteers handles information you provide.

Legal review required. The wording below is provided as a starting draft for client and legal review. It is not legal advice and has not been certified as compliant with any specific data protection regime. Final wording must be approved before publication.

1. Information We Collect

BRPV may collect the following information through the forms on this website:

  • Endorsement form: first name, surname, gender, National Identification Number (NIN), district, optional message, and consent.
  • Membership form: first name, last name, phone, email, district, sub-county, area of interest, optional message, and consent.
  • Contact form: name, email, subject, and message.

2. Purpose of Collection

Information is collected for the purposes of:

  • Registering voluntary endorsements through BRPV
  • Processing volunteer membership applications
  • Responding to enquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Coordinating BRPV activities across the Busoga region

[Official purpose wording to be reviewed]

3. Endorsement Information

Endorsement submissions are voluntary. Submitting the endorsement form does not cast an official vote and is not connected to the Electoral Commission of Uganda. Endorsement records are not published on this website and no public list of endorsements is displayed.

4. NIN Handling

Your National Identification Number (NIN) is treated as sensitive personal information. BRPV does not:

  • display submitted NINs publicly on this website
  • store NINs in your browser (localStorage, sessionStorage, or cookies)
  • place NINs in URLs or URL parameters
  • include NINs in analytics
  • log NINs to the browser console
  • show NINs in any confirmation message

The NIN is requested as part of BRPV's endorsement registration process. It is not used to verify your voter registration and does not prove that you are a Ugandan voter.

5. Consent

Each form includes a consent checkbox. By submitting a form, you confirm that you voluntarily provide your information for the stated purpose and consent to its processing in accordance with this policy.

6. Access

You may request access to the personal information you have submitted, or request correction, by contacting BRPV using the details on our Contact page.
