Disclaimer

Important information about BRPV and the content on this website.

1. About BRPV

Busoga Region Patriotic Volunteers (BRPV) is a regional patriotic volunteer and mobilisation initiative associated with the Patriotic League of Uganda (PLU). BRPV is a regional organisation focused on the Busoga region.

2. Voluntary Endorsements

Endorsement submissions made through this website are voluntary. Submitting an endorsement through BRPV does not cast an official vote and does not register a vote with any official electoral body.

3. Not the Electoral Commission

BRPV does not represent, act on behalf of, or have any official connection to the Electoral Commission of Uganda. The endorsement process on this website is an organisational endorsement registration — not an official election ballot.

4. NIN Does Not Verify Voter Status

The National Identification Number (NIN) requested on the endorsement form is collected as part of BRPV's endorsement registration process. It does not verify your voter registration and does not prove that you are a Ugandan voter.

5. Basis of Information

Information published on this website is based on officially supplied organisational information. Where official information has not yet been supplied, clearly labelled placeholders are used. BRPV does not fabricate statistics, achievements, events, biographies or organisational claims.

6. Content Updates

Content on this website may be updated, corrected or removed as official information becomes available. BRPV reserves the right to update any section of this website without prior notice.

7. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites. BRPV is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

8. Contact
